Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you register for the free live session we collect your name, mobile number, email address, the course you are interested in and, optionally, your current education. We also record basic campaign information (such as the advertisement or link you came from) to understand which of our ads are useful.

How we use it

Your information will only be used to contact you about the training session and related course information — for example, to share the session date, time and joining link, and to answer your questions about the course and admission process.

WhatsApp

We contact you on WhatsApp only if you opt in on the form or message us there first. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Sharing & retention

We do not sell your personal data. It is stored with the service providers we use to run registrations and is kept only as long as needed for these purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us at info@codingnowai.in.

Cookies

With your consent, this page uses analytics and advertising cookies (Google Analytics / Google Ads) to measure visits and registrations from our ads. If you reject them, these cookies are not set. You can change your choice using “Cookie settings” in the page footer.

Terms & Conditions

  • The free 2-hour live online session is a demonstration and introduction to the selected course. It is not the complete paid course.
  • Registration does not create any obligation to enrol in a paid course.
  • Session joining details are personal and should not be shared publicly.
  • Participants are expected to behave respectfully during the live session. The trainer may remove participants who disrupt the session.
  • Information about the full course curriculum, fees and admission is provided separately by the institute.

Session Rescheduling Policy

Session dates and times may change due to trainer availability or technical reasons. If a session is rescheduled or cancelled, registered students will be informed through the contact details they provided. If you cannot attend, contact us and we will let you know about upcoming sessions for your course, where available.

Do you provide placement support?

Yes. We provide 100% placement assistance, including resume building, mock interviews, portfolio preparation and job referrals.

Are classes live or recorded?

Classes are live and interactive with mentors, and sessions are also recorded so you can revise anytime.

Can beginners join AI & Full Stack courses?

Absolutely. Our courses are beginner-friendly and designed step by step, from the basics to an advanced level.

Will I work on real projects?

Yes. Students build real-world projects and industry-level applications to gain practical experience.

What is the course duration?

It depends on the program. Most programs run between 3 and 9 months, with flexible learning schedules.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes — the demo class is completely free, with no commitment. Book it from any “Free Demo Class” button and our counsellor will confirm your slot.

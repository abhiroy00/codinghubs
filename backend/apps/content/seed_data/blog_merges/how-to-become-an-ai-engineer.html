Salary Reality Check: AI Engineer Pay in India and Delhi NCR (2026)

AI engineering pays a premium over general software and data roles at every stage. But the range is wide, and the top of the range comes from product companies and GCCs, not services firms.

Delhi NCR specific: Average AI/ML engineer pay in Delhi NCR is ₹22–30 LPA across all experience levels. For freshers, job postings show a range from ₹8–12 LPA at a Noida-based GenAI role, ₹5.4–7.7 LPA at TCS New Delhi, and ₹6–8 LPA at a Delhi computer vision startup. Ambitionbox reports an average starting salary of around ₹12.4 LPA for AI engineers in New Delhi, with the highest reaching ₹30.8 LPA.

The honest caveat: Many people with “AI engineer” titles at services firms earn ₹7–12 LPA, which is why Glassdoor's overall average (about ₹12 LPA) looks much lower than the numbers on LinkedIn. The market rewards demonstrable skill and deployed projects, not titles or certificates.

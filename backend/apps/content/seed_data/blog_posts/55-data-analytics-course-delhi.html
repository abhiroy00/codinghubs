What does a data analyst do?

A data analyst answers business questions with data. Typical tasks include cleaning sales or customer data, writing SQL queries, building dashboards that show trends, and presenting findings to managers. For example: "Which products sell best in which months, and where are we losing customers?"

The core skills to learn

A simple SQL example

Here is the kind of query analysts write daily: total sales by city, highest first.

SELECT city, SUM(amount) AS total_sales
FROM orders
WHERE order_date >= '2026-01-01'
GROUP BY city
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;

Portfolio projects for data analysts

  • A sales dashboard showing monthly trends, top products and regions.
  • A customer churn analysis with clear recommendations.
  • An analysis of a public Indian dataset, such as air quality or transport data.

Present each with the business question, your approach, a dashboard screenshot and the conclusions.

How to choose a data analytics course

  • The syllabus covers Excel, SQL, Python and a BI tool, not just one of them.
  • You work on real datasets and finish with portfolio projects.
  • Mentors have practical analytics experience.
  • There is interview preparation, especially SQL practice.
  • The format, schedule and fees are clear and in writing.

===FAQ=== Q: What is taught in a data analytics course? A: A complete data analytics course usually covers Excel, statistics basics, SQL, Python with pandas, and a dashboard tool such as Power BI or Tableau, along with portfolio projects. Q: Can a B.Com graduate do a data analytics course? A: Yes. Commerce graduates often do well in data analytics because they understand business and finance data. No programming background is required to start. Q: How long does it take to learn data analytics? A: With regular study, many learners become ready for entry-level analyst roles in about four to eight months. Q: Is SQL or Python more important for data analysts? A: Both matter, but SQL is used in almost every analyst role and interview, so learn it early. Python is valuable for larger datasets and automation.

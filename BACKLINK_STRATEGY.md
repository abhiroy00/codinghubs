## Execution rules and measurement

Prepare a short personalized pitch explaining the audience benefit, exercise quality and sources. Measure relevant referral engagement, earned mentions and qualified enquiries; do not use raw link counts as a guarantee of ranking. Avoid PBNs, paid spam, comment automation, irrelevant directories and manipulative exchanges. No messages, posts, accounts, purchases or external profile edits were made. Outreach remains explicitly pending authorization.
